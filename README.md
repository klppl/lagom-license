# The Lagom License

*Not too much, not too little — just the right amount of freedom.*

An open-source license for software that doesn't try too hard.

**Website:** [klppl.github.io/lagom-license](https://klppl.github.io/lagom-license/)

## What is Lagom?

**Lagom** (Swedish, adj.) — not too much, not too little. Just right. (The license has a much longer definition. It knows.)

Most licenses are either too restrictive or too permissive. The Lagom License finds the balance: you can use, modify, and distribute the software — just credit the author (but not in 40pt bold font), include the license, and keep things sensible.

## The Gist

- **Use it** — for anything, commercial or otherwise
- **Share it** — original or modified, no parade required
- **Modify it** — as much as you like
- **Credit the author** — a small acknowledgment is enough *(required)*
- **Include the license** — it's just polite *(required)*
- **No warranty** — if it breaks, that is unfortunate, but expected

That's the whole deal. Two actual rules. Everything else is advice, such as:

- Don't power a galactic empire with it. A modest regional empire, at most.
- Don't modify it so much that the author feels inadequate.
- Roam freely, leave no trace, close the gate behind you.
- Respect the queue ticket.
- No support in July.

## Usage

Copy the [LICENSE](LICENSE) file into your project and replace the copyright line at the bottom with your own name. That's it. Don't overthink it.

Coffee, cinnamon buns, and a calm discussion. Failing that, a passive-aggressive note in the laundry room. Litigation is never lagom.
