§ 01

Definition

lagom

/ˈlɑ̂ːɡɔm/ adj., adv. · Swedish

  1. Not too much, not too little.
  2. Sufficient; adequate; appropriate.
  3. Just right.

Etymology Possibly from laget om, “around the team” — Vikings passing a horn of mead around the circle, each taking exactly enough that everyone got some. Etymologists consider this unlikely. Swedes consider it beside the point.

Lagom is…

  • the amount of milk in your coffee that does not need to be discussed.
  • the volume of a conversation on a commuter train, which is none.
  • a salary that is fine.
  • a summer house that is red.
  • a political opinion held quietly.
  • an applause that ends at the same time for everyone.
  • the second cinnamon bun, but never the third.
  • the Goldilocks zone, but with better furniture and meatballs.

It cannot be measured, only felt. To ask how much is lagom? is itself slightly un‑lagom.

§ 03

The fine print, which is lovely

Allemansrätten · The right to roam

Roam freely through the codebase, as through any Swedish forest.

Pick berries (snippets) and mushrooms (helper functions). No warranty that the mushrooms aren’t poisonous. Leave no trace. Don’t camp in one file for more than one night. Close the gate behind you.

Nummerlapp · The queue
Nu betjänas 042

Issues are handled in order of their queue ticket number.

The only truly unforgivable act. The Author will not say anything about it. The Author will think about it, at length, for years.

Jantelagen

You shall not think you are better than this Software.
The Software does not think it is better than you.
Nobody here thinks they are anything special.

This card is exactly as big as it needs to be. That is the point.

Assembly instructions

Ships flat-packed. The instructions are wordless and feature a small cartoon person who seems calmer than you.

Support hours

No support in July. All of July.

Issues filed in July will be read in August, by an Author with a tan and no memory of having written this code.

Releases · Lördagsgodis

Breaking changes on Saturdays, in moderation.

A small bag. Not the big bag.

If this code deletes your database, burns your toast, causes an international incident, or collides with a moose, that is between you and your backup strategy.

The Author has no control over moose. Nobody does.

Termination

Paused, not revoked.

The Lagom Clause

Using this software too much? Step away. Have a fika.

Return when balance has been restored. Using it too little? Use it a little more. Not too much.
